Why Perkins, Why Now

A brand people come back to. For sixty years and counting.

Most opportunities ask you to bet on something brand-new. Perkins is the opposite — a comfort-food name with nearly 300 restaurants and guests who've been coming for decades. We've paired that loyalty with a modern model: flexible footprints that lower the cost of getting in, and more ways than ever to earn.

We're idealistic about preserving what was, and wide open to what's next. The humble pancake house from the heartland is now a national brand made for how America eats today. Same soul, new attitude.

The Path to Ownership

Eight steps from curious to open.

Introduction call

Exploratory call
